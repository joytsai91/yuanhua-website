Battor brand, Formosa lubricants) and exclusive Taiwan distributor of CosmoTech (Japan) waste-fluid treatment
equipment; also offers waste oil / wastewater collection & transport. Readers: plant managers, purchasing and
engineers at machining shops and overseas partners. Tone: clear, confident, professional, plain English (US
spelling). Natural English, never word-for-word; keep it concise — English UI text should not be much longer
than necessary.

## Writing rules
- Product names are Title Case ("Energy-Saving Hydraulic Oil", "Formosa Gear Oil GII"); headings use sentence case.
- Full-width punctuation becomes ASCII: ／ → " / ", （） → (), ：→ ": ", 、→ ", ", 「」→ quotes, ～ → "–", ＊ → "*", ・/． → " / " or "·".
- No Chinese characters may remain in any translation.
- Old-site product copy sometimes has typos or garbled phrases: translate the evident intended meaning in correct
  technical English; when the meaning is genuinely unclear, translate conservatively and report it in your notes.
  Do not add facts that are not in the source, and do not drop facts that are.

## Product & series names

How to read the tables: on the Chinese side, " / " (ASCII) separates variants and "／" (full-width) is part of the source text. Variants map to the English items in the same order; a single English item means all variants take it. Where an English item itself contains "/", variants are separated by ";".

Patterns (apply everywhere — titles, meta descriptions, breadcrumbs, contact-form prefill):
- Page titles `X｜Battor 油品系列` → `X | Battor Series`; `X｜台塑 潤滑油系列` → `X | Formosa Lubricants`; `X｜SAN-I Yushiro 油品系列` → `X | SAN-I Yushiro Series`;
  `X｜SAN-I Yushiro 植物性油品` → `X | SAN-I Yushiro Plant-Based Oils`; `X｜廢液(水)處理裝置` → `X | Wastewater Treatment Equipment`; `X｜展覽花絮` → `X | Exhibition Highlights`.
- Meta descriptions `Battor 油品系列－X：…` → `Battor Series – X: …` (same for `台塑 潤滑油系列－` → `Formosa Lubricants – `, `SAN-I Yushiro 油品系列－` → `SAN-I Yushiro Series – `, `SAN-I Yushiro 植物性油品－` → `SAN-I Yushiro Plant-Based Oils – `).
  `CosmoTech <equipment type> <model>：…` → `CosmoTech <Equipment Type> <model>: …` (e.g. "CosmoTech Water-Soluble Waste Fluid Treatment System FRIENDLY CT-AQ25H: …").
- Spec headings `<1>n</1><type><2><category> (SAN-I Yushiro 油品)</2>` → `<1>n</1><Type><2><Category> (SAN-I Yushiro)</2>`, e.g. `<1>1</1>水溶性<2>添加劑 (SAN-I Yushiro 油品)</2>` → `<1>1</1>Water-Soluble<2>Additives (SAN-I Yushiro)</2>`.
- "系列" after a model/series code → "Series" (e.g. "HIVIS Fluid DU 系列" → "HIVIS Fluid DU Series"). In running text "台塑…油 GII 系列" → "Formosa … Oil GII series".
- Contact-form prefill units (`item=` / `&topic=`): translate the product name exactly as in this table; the value after `&topic=` must be **identical** to the English contact-form option it selects (`廢水處理設備` → `Wastewater Treatment Equipment`, `廢油・廢水清運` → `Waste Oil & Wastewater Collection`), e.g. `DR-1&topic=廢水處理設備` → `DR-1&topic=Wastewater Treatment Equipment`.
- Latin model codes, grade codes and source spellings stay exactly as written (GII, Group II, Group 2, AW, RC, SC, TB, HD, DU, HIVIS, HLPSO, ISO grades, CT-AQ…), including the old-site spellings "HEAT BRANFER", "Refigerator", "EMD Fluid".

## Spec-table & FAQ vocabulary

General rules: `.C`, `ﾟ C`, `度C` → "°C"; `mm/s2`, `mm/s`, `mm2/s` (viscosity) → "mm²/s"; `um` → "µm"; `hr` → "h"; `l`/`ℓ` (litres) → "L"; `約` → "approx."; `三相` / `3相` → "3-phase"; `倍` (dilution) → "×" (e.g. "10-30倍" → "10–30×"); `N個月` → "N months"; keep `<br>` and all tags.
Formosa spec-heading tags `<2>APPLIANCE</2>`, `<2>CLASSIC DATA</2>` etc. are already English — leave their content unchanged.
