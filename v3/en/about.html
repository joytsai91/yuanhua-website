About Us

From metalworking oils,always on the shop floor

The product is only the starting point. What really matters is whether it meets the machining needs on your shop floor.

Yuan Hua Oil has long specialized in metalworking and industrial oils. Starting from oil supply, we have built up experience in product application, field service and solving process problems. As customer needs have grown from "choosing the right oil" to usage management, fluid changes, wastewater treatment and follow-up collection, Yuan Hua has kept expanding its services to offer more complete answers to the real problems manufacturing sites face.

Understanding the Shop Floor

Starting with oil,we learn what the shop floor needs

The problems metalworking shops face are rarely just about whether an oil meets spec.

Workpiece material, machines and equipment, cutting tools, machining methods, water quality, working concentration and daily management can all affect cutting fluid performance, machining quality and downstream waste fluid treatment.

That's why, beyond supplying products, Yuan Hua pays close attention to actual operating conditions and what is happening on site. From understanding machining needs and product use to downstream treatment, we aim to make every product choice a closer fit for the problems customers actually face.

Expanding Our Services

Wherever customer needs lead,our services keep pace

Yuan Hua started out in metalworking oils, but over years of serving customers we came to see that their real problems don't end at product delivery. Once an oil is in use, several other stages can still come into play: how it performs, concentration control, oil and coolant changes, waste fluid treatment and follow-up collection.

In response, Yuan Hua has gradually expanded from product supply into field service, wastewater treatment equipment and downstream treatment. Our aim is to reduce the communication costs customers face when dealing with multiple suppliers and to create a more seamless link between oil use and end-of-process treatment.

  1. 01

    Oils & product application

    From cutting fluids and cutting oils to all kinds of industrial oils, we help evaluate options based on your actual needs.

  2. 02

    On-site use & technical support

    We provide follow-up service and support based on how each product is implemented and performs in use.

  3. 03

    Equipment & downstream treatment

    As needed, our services extend to wastewater treatment equipment, oil and coolant changes, and waste oil / wastewater collection.

Brands & Technology

Built on products and technology,we keep expanding our services
