Battor Series

Air Compressor Oil

GIREX SW NG ISO: 32-680

Features

Lead-free extreme pressure (EP) gear oil that resists water, foam, wear and corrosion and markedly reduces friction and noise. Suitable for reduction gears, heavy-duty gears, general thrust ball bearings and similar applications, and for industrial gears running at oil temperatures above 100 °C. MG contains molybdenum disulfide. Meets FZG load stage 12 requirements and DIN 51517 CFLP. SCREW RA.HS air compressor oil is highly refined, with outstanding oxidation stability, for reciprocating and screw air compressors operating from -10 °C to 220 °C. AS is a synthetic type; oil change every 8000 hours.

Metalworking, lubrication and cooling applications for all kinds of CNC machinery and industrial machines.
