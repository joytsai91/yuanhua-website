Equipment overview

In recent years, machining and many other operations have adopted water-soluble fluids for better safety, cooling performance and working conditions.
These water-soluble fluids are usually diluted about 5–10× with water, and reportedly 90% of the metalworking fluids in use today are water-soluble. However, the oily waste fluid left after use is hard to treat, and most of it currently ends up as industrial waste, releasing large amounts of carbon dioxide when incinerated.

Using proprietary membrane separation technology, FRIENDLY-UF efficiently concentrates and separates water-soluble oily waste fluid, sharply reducing its volume and supporting both environmental protection and cost reduction.

Source: CosmoTech (cosmotech-jp)

Drastically reduces waste fluid discharge

Features

  • Efficiently concentrates and separates emulsified oil without adsorbents, coagulants or similar agents (99% removal).
  • Waste fluid can be concentrated to 1/10–1/20 of its original volume. *Varies with the type and concentration of the waste fluid.
  • Concentrating and separating the waste fluid substantially reduces industrial waste disposal costs.
  • The clean permeate can be recovered and reused, which is both economical and eco-friendly.
  • The separation membrane can be cleaned, so it lasts a long time.
  • Separation with a special membrane generates no sludge.
  • Fully automatic operation that anyone can run with ease.

How the separation membrane works

Proprietary membrane technology for efficient separation of oily waste fluid

Oily waste fluid is passed through a separation membrane bonded to the inside of porous fibers, which lets only water (including low-molecular-weight substances and ions) permeate and separates it from the oil (including high-molecular-weight substances and suspended matter). Highly turbulent flow inside the membrane maintains a high permeation rate without clogging the pores, ensuring stable treatment. The membrane is also kept clean by regular automatic cleaning with sponge balls or, when needed, by chemical cleaning. This membrane offers excellent heat and chemical resistance, making it suitable for a wide variety of applications.

As the feed fluid passes through the separation membrane, only water (including low-molecular-weight substances and ions) permeates, and the concentrate returns to the feed tank.
By continuously circulating the feed and drawing off only the permeate, the feed is concentrated to 1/10–1/20 of its original volume. *
As permeate is discharged, the level in the feed tank drops; operation ends when the target level is reached.

* Varies with the type and concentration of the waste fluid.

Application

Effectively treats waste fluids discharged from a wide range of machining and forming processes.

Spent cleaning fluid, spent cutting fluid, spent grinding fluid, compressor drain water, car wash wastewater, casting waste fluid, high-frequency (induction) coolant wastewater, rolling coolant wastewater, etc.
