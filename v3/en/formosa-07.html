Formosa Lubricants

Formosa Gear Oil GII

API Group II

Product Properties

Formosa Gear Oil Group II series is a high-performance gear oil developed for heavy-duty industrial gear systems. Made with highly refined base oils meeting API Group II standards, it offers better oxidation resistance and thermal stability than conventional mineral-oil gear oils, while specially selected EP and anti-wear additives effectively protect gears and reduce wear for superior gear performance.

Product Specifications

Recommended ApplicationsPerformance FeaturesHealth, Safety & Environmental InformationSpecifications & Approvals

1Recommended ApplicationsAPPLIANCE

• General heavy-duty industrial gear systems.
• Bearing systems running at low speeds under heavy loads.

2Performance FeaturesFUNCTIONS & FEATURES

• Group 2 base oil: Formosa Gear Oil HD series uses highly refined base oils meeting API Group II standards, giving better oxidation resistance and thermal stability than conventional mineral-oil gear oils. It suits hot, demanding operating environments, resists degradation and sludge formation, and lasts longer.
• Extreme pressure (EP) and anti-wear performance: Even under extremely severe conditions such as long operating hours and heavy loads, Formosa Gear Oil Group II series effectively prevents wear on gear surfaces of copper and other metal alloys, protecting gears and bearings.
• Low friction: Reduces the heat generated by gear friction, avoiding wasted energy and increasing power output and productivity.
• Demulsibility and rust and corrosion protection: Fast oil-water separation is essential for gear oils, especially in steel mills and other environments where damp conditions are unavoidable. Excellent demulsibility and rust and corrosion protection reduce the chance of gear components rusting or corroding.
• Strong antifoam performance: Selected antifoam additives release air quickly and resist foaming.

3Health, Safety & Environmental InformationHEALTH & ENVIRONMENTAL INFO

According to the current product data sheet, this product has no adverse effects on health or safety when used as recommended; however, the instructions in the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) must still be followed during use. The MSDS and related information are available from your local distributor. Dispose of used oil through licensed recycling channels and never discharge it into sewers, soil or rivers. Please help protect the environment.

3Specifications & ApprovalsCERTIFICATES

Formosa Gear Oil HD meets the following standards:

• US Steel 224
• DIN 51517- Part 3 (CLP)

Applications

Metalworking, lubrication and cooling applications for all kinds of CNC machinery and industrial machines.
