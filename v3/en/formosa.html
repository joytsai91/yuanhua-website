About the Series

Lubricant specialists (industrial lubricants): to give customers in Taiwan a wider choice of lubricants, Yuan Hua Oil carries Formosa Lubricants. Formosa Petrochemical officially entered the domestic market in 1999 and launched its lubricant products in 2001, building out a more comprehensive lubricant service and industrial lubricant range. Formosa Lubricants were at first used only within the group and sold at its gas stations; after ten years of building up technology and experience, the brand began a full-scale entry into markets in Taiwan and abroad in 2011.

As Taiwan's only lubricant supplier that also produces base oil, Formosa has a stable supply of high-quality base oils, which it combines with advanced formulation technology and excellent manufacturing capability to develop high-quality lubricants for a wide range of industrial equipment and transport vehicles. To serve the Taiwan market in depth, it has spent years developing new products and improving performance to meet increasingly demanding and diverse market needs, backed by a comprehensive, close-knit dealer network that gives customers more responsive service.

Today the Formosa Lubricants line covers industrial, heavy-duty vehicle, marine and passenger car applications. Made in Taiwan to international quality standards, Formosa Lubricants serve the people of this beautiful island, so that every trade and industry, from large companies to individuals, can find the Formosa lubricant it needs. Looking ahead, the brand aims to expand worldwide and become Taiwan's pride in the lubricant industry!
