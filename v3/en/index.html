About Yuan Hua

We do more than supply oil —we start with real problems.

Machining sites need more than oils that simply meet specifications. Cutting fluid odor, oxidation, short service life, tool wear, workpiece rust and downstream wastewater treatment are ongoing challenges on the shop floor.

Yuan Hua starts with oil selection and technical service, helps evaluate options against your actual machining conditions, and extends to oil and coolant changes, wastewater treatment equipment and follow-up collection — so every product truly serves the needs of the shop floor.
