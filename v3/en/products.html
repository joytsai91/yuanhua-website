01 / Plant-Based

SAN-I Yushiro Plant-Based Oils

Plant-based esters are extracted from vegetable oils. Their oil film resists breakdown and provides high lubricity, meeting the demands of high-precision machining under severe conditions without chlorinated paraffin EP agents.

Formosa Lubricants
