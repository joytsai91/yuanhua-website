SAN-I Yushiro Series

Water-Soluble Cutting Fluids

Synthetic · Semi-Synthetic / Emulsion / Chemical (Solution)

This series includes:

Water-soluble cutting fluids (also called water-based cutting oils) provide highly effective cooling for industrial machinery. Yuan Hua offers synthetic / semi-synthetic, emulsion-type and chemical (solution)-type water-soluble cutting fluids. Applications: CNC machinery and all kinds of industrial machines.
